What to expect

🍂 Fall color

October 9–11 is late for some high-country aspens. Remaining foliage is a bonus; the alpine scenery still carries the trip.

❄️ Mountain weather

Pack layers, gloves and a shell. RMNP and Independence Pass can feel wintry even when Denver is comfortable.

🏔 Altitude

Independence Pass exceeds 12,000 ft. Hydrate, move slowly and keep the summit stop easy.

🚗 Seasonal-road fallback

If Independence Pass closes, do not force the route. Use the safer open-road route to Aspen and preserve Maroon Bells/Glenwood/Vail.
