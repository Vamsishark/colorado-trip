# 🏔️ Colorado Fall Road Trip 2026

An interactive travel planner for our **3-day Colorado fall road trip — October 9–11, 2026**.

The route is optimized to visit **Rocky Mountain National Park first** and continue through Colorado without repeatedly returning to Denver.

## 🌐 Live Trip Planner

👉 **https://vamsishark.github.io/colorado-trip/**

---

# 🗺️ Optimized Route

```text
Denver
  ↓

  ↓
Estes Park
  ↓
Twin Lakes
  ↓
Independence Pass
  ↓
Aspen
  ↓
Maroon Bells
  ↓

  ↓

```

### Complete Route

**Denver → RMNP → Estes Park → Twin Lakes → Independence Pass → Aspen → Maroon Bells → Glenwood Springs → Vail → Denver**

The trip is designed as one continuous loop to reduce unnecessary backtracking.

---

# 📅 Day 1 — Friday, October 9

## 🦌 Denver → Rocky Mountain National Park → Estes Park

### Early Morning

**5:45 AM — Leave Denver**

Drive toward Rocky Mountain National Park.

### 🏔️ Rocky Mountain National Park

Target arrival around **7:30 AM**.

Main stops:

- Bear Lake
- Nymph Lake
- Dream Lake
- Emerald Lake — optional
- Wildlife viewing
- Scenic mountain viewpoints

### 🥾 Main Hike

```text
Bear Lake
   ↓
Nymph Lake
   ↓
Dream Lake
   ↓
Emerald Lake (optional)
```

Dream Lake is the main target.

Continue to Emerald Lake only if the weather, trail conditions and energy levels are good.

### 🦌 Wildlife

October is a great time to watch for elk around RMNP and Estes Park.

Always maintain a safe distance from wildlife.

### 🏘️ Estes Park

After RMNP:

- Lunch
- Explore downtown
- Scenic views
- Dinner
- Relax after hiking

### 🛏️ Overnight

**Estes Park**

We do **not return to Denver**, keeping the trip moving west.

---

# 📅 Day 2 — Saturday, October 10

## 🍂 Estes Park → Twin Lakes → Independence Pass → Aspen → Maroon Bells

This is the main **high-alpine scenic day**.

### Morning

**~6:30 AM — Leave Estes Park**

Drive toward Twin Lakes.

### 🌊 Twin Lakes

Short scenic stop.

Recommended:

- Lake viewpoints
- Mountain photography
- Quick break before Independence Pass

### 🏔️ Independence Pass

Elevation:

**12,095 ft**

Highlights:

- Continental Divide
- High-alpine scenery
- Mountain overlooks
- Scenic drive
- Photography

⚠️ Independence Pass is seasonal and weather-dependent.

Check road conditions before leaving:

**https://www.cotrip.org/**

### 🍂 Aspen

Continue down Independence Pass toward Aspen.

Use Aspen for:

- Lunch
- Hotel/luggage drop
- Downtown walk
- Fall scenery

### 🌄 Maroon Bells

Main afternoon destination.

Highlights:

- Maroon Lake
- Maroon Bells viewpoints
- Photography
- Easy scenic walking
- Remaining fall colors

Reservation/access information:

**https://www.visitmaroonbells.com/**

### 🛏️ Overnight

**Aspen**

Staying downtown or close to downtown makes dinner and evening walking easier.

---

# 📅 Day 3 — Sunday, October 11

## ♨️ Aspen → Glenwood Springs → Glenwood Canyon → Vail → Denver

This is the more relaxed final sightseeing day.

### Morning

**~8:00 AM — Leave Aspen**

Drive toward Glenwood Springs.

### ♨️ Glenwood Hot Springs

Spend approximately **2–3 hours** relaxing at the hot springs.

Great recovery after the previous two mountain days.

### 🍽️ Glenwood Springs

Lunch before continuing east.

### 🏞️ Glenwood Canyon

Drive east on I-70 through Glenwood Canyon.

The drive itself is part of the sightseeing.

Do not stop on highway shoulders for photos.

### 🏘️ Vail

Spend approximately **1.5–2 hours** exploring:

- Vail Village
- Gore Creek
- Mountain architecture
- Coffee
- Restaurants
- Shopping

### 🚗 Vail → Denver

Leave Vail in the late afternoon.

Continue directly toward Denver.

### 🛏️ Overnight

**Denver International Airport area**

This makes the next morning much easier.

---

# ✈️ Monday, October 12

## Flight Home

**Flight: 10:00 AM from Denver International Airport**

Aim to reach DEN around:

**7:30–8:00 AM**

Especially if returning a rental car.

No sightseeing is planned for this morning.

# 🗺️ Map Design

The trip overview uses an **embedded SVG route map**.

This avoids dependency on:

- Map API keys
- External map tile servers
- JavaScript mapping libraries

The overview therefore works reliably on GitHub Pages.

For actual driving directions, the app provides direct **Google Maps navigation links** for every major route segment.

# ⚠️ Travel Notes

Colorado mountain weather can change quickly in October.

Before each mountain day, check:

- Weather
- Road conditions
- Park alerts
- Trail conditions
- Reservation status

**Independence Pass is the most weather-sensitive part of this itinerary.**

If the pass closes because of snow or unsafe conditions, use an alternate open route to Aspen rather than attempting to cross the pass.
